How I work

Design decisions get tested on a stage, not just in a Figma file.

Listen

Shadow the user's actual set

I sit in on rehearsals and load-ins before I open a design file. Most usability problems in music software show up under time pressure, in the dark, with gloves on.

Build

Prototype in the real material

A tap target that works on a laptop trackpad often fails on a touchscreen bolted to a pedalboard. I prototype on the actual hardware as early as possible.

Ship

Design for the hundredth show

Novelty wears off after one tour. I optimize for the version of the interface a tired musician can operate on instinct, six months in.

About

I started out repairing synths in a shop in Lagos before I ever touched a design tool — which is probably why I still think about interfaces the way I think about circuits: what has to happen in what order, and what breaks first when someone's in a hurry.

These days I split my time between product design and playing bass in a five-piece that gigs most weekends, which keeps me honest about which of my own designs actually survive a real set.
